Our Story

Bringing the Ancient Art of Finnish Sauna Culture to Modern Britain

Authentic barrel sauna craftsmanship

Crafted for Wellness, Built with Passion

Nomad Steam was born from a simple belief: everyone deserves access to the profound benefits of authentic sauna bathing. Having experienced the transformative power of traditional Finnish saunas, we set out to make this ancient wellness practice accessible, convenient, and unforgettable.

Our barrel saunas are handcrafted from sustainably sourced timber, featuring traditional wood-fired heating that creates the perfect dry heat environment. Each sauna can accommodate up to 6 people and reaches optimal temperatures of 70-90°C.

How It Works

Simple, Seamless, Stress-Free Sauna Experience

1

Book Your Dates

Choose your preferred dates and package. Check our availability calendar and select the option that suits you best. Instant confirmation upon booking.

Our team delivers the sauna to your location and handles complete setup. We'll position it perfectly in your garden, driveway, or chosen spot. Setup takes just 30 minutes.

We provide full instructions on lighting and maintaining the wood-fired stove. The sauna reaches optimal temperature (80°C) in approximately 45-60 minutes. Sustainably sourced wood included.

Finnish Sauna Health Benefits

Science-Backed Health & Wellness Benefits

Improved Heart Health

Regular sauna use improves cardiovascular function, increases heart rate similar to moderate exercise, and can reduce risk of heart disease.

Detoxification

Flush out body toxins via sweating. The heat opens pores and helps eliminate waste products through perspiration.

Reduced Stress & Anxiety

Heat therapy promotes relaxation, releases endorphins, and helps lower cortisol levels for better mental wellbeing.

Improved Sleep

Sauna use before bed can deepen sleep cycles, helping you fall asleep faster and enjoy more restful sleep.

Muscle Recovery

Relaxing muscles and easing aches in joints. Heat therapy improves blood circulation to speed recovery after exercise.

Decreased Dementia Risk

Studies show regular sauna bathing (4-7 times per week) can reduce risk of dementia and Alzheimer's disease.

Immune System Boost

Regular sauna use stimulates white blood cell production and strengthens your body's natural defenses against illness.

Calorie Burning

Sauna sessions can burn 300-600 calories per hour through increased metabolism and heart rate.

Absolutely! Safety is our top priority. Each sauna comes with comprehensive safety equipment including a fire extinguisher, carbon monoxide detector, and detailed safety instructions. We provide a full briefing during setup, covering proper wood-burning techniques, ventilation, and usage guidelines. Our saunas are built to strict safety standards and are covered by our liability insurance. We require a 2-meter clearance from buildings and proper ventilation during use.

Children aged 12 and above can use the sauna with adult supervision. We recommend shorter sessions (5-10 minutes) and lower temperatures for younger users. Children should be closely monitored at all times. Pregnant women, people with heart conditions, or those with medical concerns should consult their GP before using a sauna.

We recommend booking at least 2-3 weeks in advance, especially for weekend dates during peak season (April-September). However, we can often accommodate last-minute bookings subject to availability. For events and weddings, we suggest booking 2-3 months ahead. Check our live availability calendar for real-time booking slots.

Bring towels for sitting on and drying off, plenty of water for hydration, and swimwear (though private use allows for traditional Finnish nude bathing if preferred). We recommend having a cold shower or outdoor cooling area nearby. Optional extras include robes, slippers, and refreshments. Our premium packages include towels and aromatherapy oils.

We offer free delivery within 15 miles of our Oxfordshire base. For distances between 15-30 miles, there's a £25 delivery charge. We can arrange delivery further afield for longer hire periods or events - please contact us for a custom quote. We cover most of South Central England including Oxfordshire, Gloucestershire, Berkshire, Buckinghamshire, and Wiltshire.

The sauna is fully weatherproof and designed for outdoor use in all conditions! In fact, many people find the contrast between hot sauna and cool rain absolutely exhilarating - it's a very authentic Nordic experience. The sauna interior stays completely dry, and the wood-fired stove continues to work perfectly in any weather.

From lighting the stove, the sauna typically reaches optimal temperature (70-80°C) in 45-60 minutes depending on outside temperature and weather conditions. We provide detailed instructions on building and maintaining the fire. Once at temperature, it's easy to maintain the heat by adding wood every 30-45 minutes. The thermal mass of the wood barrel helps retain heat beautifully.
